Bot Strike: Dust II
===================

A 5v5 bots game that plays from your own copy of Counter-Strike 2.
Dust II, the agents, the guns, their sounds, the HUD icons, the menu music and the fonts are read
from your Counter-Strike 2 install on first launch and cached on your PC. None of Counter-Strike 2's
files are included in this download.

Controls
--------
WASD move, SPACE jump, CTRL crouch, SHIFT walk, mouse aim/shoot, right mouse AWP scope,
1/2/3 weapons, 5 bomb, G drop bomb, R reload, B buy menu, E defuse, TAB scores, ESC pause.
Build Fight mode: Q wall, X floor, C ramp, left mouse to place, 1/2/3 back to your guns.
