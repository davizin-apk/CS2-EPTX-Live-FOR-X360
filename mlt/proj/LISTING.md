# Bot Strike: Dust II

**Tagline:** 5v5 vs bots on Dust II from your own CS2: play it with Counter-Strike 1.6 rules, build like Fortnite mid-fight, or buy a Portal Gun.

## What you get

Bot Strike is a 5v5 game against bots, built from your own copy of Counter-Strike 2. The first time you
start it, it reads Dust II, the SAS and Phoenix agents, the guns, their sounds, the HUD icons and the
menu music from your CS2 install. After that it starts in seconds. Nothing from CS2 is in the download.

- **Classic 1.6 vs Bots** – Dust II with Counter-Strike 1.6 rules on CS2's agents and guns: 250 u/s
  knife runs, bunny hopping and air strafing (jump with Space or mouse wheel down), 1.6 recoil that
  kicks your view up and sideways, 1.6 prices ($2500 AK, $3100 M4 with 30 rounds), 105-second rounds,
  first to 16, and the old amber HUD.
- **Competitive vs Bots** – you and 4 bots against 5 bots. Bomb defusal on Dust II, first to 7 rounds,
  sides swap at halftime. You get buy time, money for wins, losses and kills, and a buy menu.
- **Build Fight vs Bots** – the same match with Fortnite building: walls, floors, stairs and cones
  (Q, X, C, V or F1-F4) on a 5.12 m grid, in wood, brick or metal, with turbo building, edits (F) and
  pieces that fall when nothing holds them up. Bots wall up when you shoot at them.
- **Portal Strike vs Bots** – buy a Portal Gun. Fire the blue portal at a wall or floor, then open the
  map with right mouse and drop the orange one anywhere; the map shows where the enemies are heading
  and recommends spots. You, the bots and every bullet go through.
- The main menu shows your agent standing on Dust II. Pick your side and bot difficulty (Easy,
  Normal or Hard).
- Weapons: Knife, Glock-18, USP-S, AK-47, M4A1-S, AWP, the Portal Gun (Portal Strike), plus Kevlar,
  a helmet and a defuse kit.
- Single player, offline. It never starts or changes Counter-Strike 2 itself.

## Controls

WASD move · Space jump · Ctrl crouch · Shift walk · Mouse aim/shoot · Right mouse AWP scope ·
1/2/3 weapons · 4 Portal Gun · 5 bomb · G drop bomb · R reload · B buy · E defuse · Tab scores · Esc pause ·
Build Fight: Q wall, X floor, C stairs, V cone, left mouse to place (hold for turbo), right mouse material, F edit.
